## CASE STUDY 1: LUMINA — Luxury Chronographs & Fine Gem Staging
**Category**: Luxury Products, Jewelry, E-Commerce Product Photography
**Client URL**: https://lumina-watches.com/
**Campaign Type**: Hero Product Shots + Meta Ad Creatives

### What Was Produced
- **W1. Hero Shot — Eclipse Diver on Obsidian Glass**: Studio-quality macro hero shot of a luxury dive watch placed on a polished obsidian glass surface with deep reflections and cinematic rim-lighting.
- **W4. Meta Ad Creative — Watch on Wrist, Urban Night**: Lifestyle wrist-shot in an urban nighttime environment for Meta (Facebook/Instagram) paid advertising.
- **Product Reference Images**: High-fidelity reference product sheets for brand alignment.

### Key Technical Challenges Solved
- Maintaining micro-detail accuracy on watch faces (sub-millimeter hands, dial text, bezel engravings).
- Creating physically accurate reflections on the obsidian glass surface (subsurface scatter, contact shadow).
- Ensuring the wrist-shot in the urban setting had consistent lighting match between the physical product and the AI environment.

### Tools Used
- Google Nano Banana 2 (hero shot generation)
- Adobe Photoshop (reflection compositing, shadow painting, grain matching)
- Canva (Meta Ad layout, typography overlay)

### Results & Proof Points
- Achieves "studio photography" standard — indistinguishable from real product photography at first glance.
- Demonstrates ability to work with ultra-precise, high-stakes product details (watch dials, gem facets).

### Best Used For Jobs Mentioning
Jewelry, watches, luxury products, high-end e-commerce, product photography, Meta Ads, premium brand aesthetics.

## CASE STUDY 4: Magento 2 & Shopify Product Ingestion Automation Pipeline
**Category**: E-Commerce Automation, AI Workflow Architecture
**Campaign Type**: Automated Product Content Generation & Publishing

### What Was Produced
A complete end-to-end automated pipeline that:
1. Scrapes raw supplier product data.
2. AI-rewrites SEO-optimized product descriptions matching specific brand voice.
3. Generates 5 high-fidelity visual renders per product automatically.
4. Auto-publishes finished products to Magento 2 and Shopify via REST APIs.

### Key Technical Challenges Solved
- Building a zero-touch pipeline from supplier data to live store product listing.
- Maintaining brand voice consistency across AI-generated product copy for hundreds of SKUs.
- Integrating multiple AI tools and APIs into a single orchestrated n8n workflow.

### Tools Used
- Python (scraping and data processing)
- n8n (workflow orchestration and API integrations)
- Flux.1 (product image rendering)
- Magento 2 REST API + Shopify Admin API (publishing)
- Airtable (job queue tracking)

### Best Used For Jobs Mentioning
E-commerce automation, Shopify, Magento, product listing, content generation, AI pipeline, workflow automation, Python, n8n.

## CASE STUDY 6: Consistent Room Viewpoint Design (Interior Architecture AI)
**Category**: Interior Design, Real Estate, AI Viewpoint Consistency
**Campaign Type**: Multi-Angle Room Design Application

### What Was Produced
A specialized ControlNet + IP-Adapter pipeline to:
1. Lock the exact geometry and perspective of a target room photo (using Depth + Canny ControlNet).
2. Inject a reference interior design style (using IP-Adapter Style Transfer).
3. Generate a new image showing the same room design from a different camera angle — without altering walls, windows, or architectural features.

### Key Technical Challenges Solved
- Preventing AI from "reinventing" the room architecture when changing viewpoints.
- Maintaining exact material textures, color palette, and furniture style across multiple angles.
- Blending the generated output seamlessly with the original room's physical features.

### Tools Used
- Google Flow / ControlNet (Depth + Canny + IP-Adapter)
- Adobe Photoshop (cleanup, perspective line correction, material finishing)
- ComfyUI / Stable Diffusion (for ControlNet pipeline access)

### Best Used For Jobs Mentioning
Interior design, room redesign, real estate staging, viewpoint consistency, multi-angle AI image generation, ControlNet, architectural visualization.
