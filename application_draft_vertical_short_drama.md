## Character-Consistency Workflow

1. **Series bible:** Build canonical character sheets covering face, body proportions, wardrobe, props, age, expressions, lighting rules, and forbidden variations. Build a matching world bible for ships, cabins, ports, weather, palette, lens language, and period details.
2. **Reference locking:** Create approved hero frames and turnaround/expression sheets using Google Nano Banana 2, Google Omni, Midjourney, or Flux. Assign immutable IDs to every character, costume, prop, and location.
3. **Shot database:** Track episode, scene, shot, character IDs, wardrobe state, geography, time of day, model/version, reference images, prompts, seeds where supported, and approval status in Airtable.
4. **Controlled generation:** Generate anchor frames before motion. Use image-to-video and first/last-frame controls in Veo, Kling, or Seedance, keeping dialogue shots short enough to preserve faces and costume details.
5. **Continuity QA:** Compare every shot against the approved reference pack; reject facial drift, handedness errors, costume changes, scale errors, ship-layout inconsistencies, and broken eyelines before editing.
6. **Finishing:** Match color, grain, motion cadence, ambience, subtitles, music, and SFX across episodes. Maintain master and platform-specific 9:16 exports through a versioned delivery checklist.
