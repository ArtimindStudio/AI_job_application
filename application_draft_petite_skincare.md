## Cover Letter

Hi there,

Your brief caught my attention — maintaining exact product accuracy while creating professional campaign-quality imagery is precisely what I specialize in.

I work with skincare and beauty brands to produce AI-generated product photography that looks indistinguishable from professional studio shoots. The key difference in my workflow versus most AI designers: I treat the original product photography as sacred. Packaging colours, label artwork, bottle shapes, logos — these are never reinterpreted or distorted.

**My process for Petite Skin Co would be:**

1. **Product Reference Mapping** — I use Google Antigravity to build a detailed reference library of your product range, ensuring every detail (pump shapes, lid colours, label text, relative sizing) is catalogued and maintained across all outputs.

2. **Scene Generation** — Using Google Nano Banana 2 and Gemini, I generate photorealistic environments (bathroom shelves, bedroom vanities, flat lay compositions, ingredient-led scenes) with precise control over lighting, shadows, and perspective.

3. **Product Compositing & Finishing** — Using Adobe Photoshop for meticulous compositing, I place your actual product photography into AI-generated scenes, then correct any AI artefacts, match lighting/colour temperature, and ensure the final image feels like genuine campaign photography.

4. **Multi-Format Delivery** — I deliver in all required dimensions (1080×1080, 1080×1440, 1080×1920) with appropriate safe zones for ad text, headlines and CTAs.

**Why this matters for your output needs:**
My AI-native workflow lets me produce 30-50+ finished images per day at campaign quality — meaning your Meta, Google, Pinterest, Instagram, website and email creative pipeline stays consistently full without sacrificing the attention to detail you need.

I've attached examples of AI product photography I've created for skincare brands, specifically showing products placed into realistic styled environments with accurate packaging maintained throughout.

I'm available 40 hours/week and happy to start with a small test batch so you can evaluate quality before committing to ongoing work.

Looking forward to hearing from you.
