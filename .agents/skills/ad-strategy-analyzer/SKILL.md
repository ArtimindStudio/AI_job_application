# Ad Strategy Analyzer

Analyze ads by breaking them into core mechanics:

1. **Hook (Visual & Audio):**
   - Visual Hook (first 3s): What's shown on screen?
   - Audio Hook: What's said/heard?
   - Why it works (psychology)

2. **Format:** UGC, Founder Story, Skit, Educational, ASMR, Unboxing, Us vs. Them, etc.

3. **Offer / Value Proposition:** What's sold and the incentive to act now.

4. **Emotional Trigger:** Core desire or pain point (status, time-saving, FOMO, insecurity, belonging).

5. **CTA:** How the ad transitions to the ask.

## Output Format
```markdown
### Ad Teardown Summary
[1-2 sentence overview]

### Core Mechanics Breakdown
- **Hook (Visual):** [Details]
- **Hook (Audio):** [Details]
- **Format:** [Details]
- **Offer:** [Details]
- **Emotional Trigger:** [Details]
- **CTA:** [Details]

### Strategist Takeaway
[How to adapt this format for our product/client]
```
