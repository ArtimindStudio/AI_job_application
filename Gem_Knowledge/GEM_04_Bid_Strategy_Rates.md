## RATE FRAMEWORK

### Dennis's Baseline Rates
| Engagement Type | Rate |
|:---|:---|
| Profile baseline (Upwork listed) | $15 USD/hr |
| Standard long-term hourly | $20–25 USD/hr |
| Specialist/Technical projects | $30–50 USD/hr |
| Strategy & consulting | $50–100+ USD/hr |
| Fixed monthly retainer | Negotiable — based on deliverable volume |

### The "Confidence Play" Rule (ALWAYS APPLY)
- **Never bid below the client's stated floor.** Bidding lower signals you are not an expert.
- **Standard bid**: Client Floor + 20–30% (e.g., client says "$20–$50/hr" → bid $25–$30)
- **Top-of-range bid**: For invited jobs or perfect-fit jobs. Bid at or near client maximum.
- **Rationale in cover letter**: Always anchor the rate to your premium tool stack and specialized expertise, not just "experience."

## RATE COUNTER-OFFER SCRIPT (When Offered a Low Rate)
Use this framework when a client proposes an unacceptably low rate:

**Key arguments to include**:
1. **Math error**: If $5/hr × 200hrs = $1,000/month, that requires 50 hrs/week — exceeding full-time.
2. **Overhead costs**: Dennis personally pays for Nano Banana 2, Midjourney Pro, Adobe CC, Kling, and other professional AI tools. These are real costs that must be covered.
3. **Specialist rate**: This is senior specialist work, not commodity labor. The rate should reflect expertise, not just time.
4. **Counter proposal**: Offer either a higher hourly rate ($20+/hr) OR a fixed monthly retainer at a reasonable deliverable volume.

**Tone**: Always diplomatic and collaborative — frame it as "ensuring long-term quality and a sustainable working relationship."
