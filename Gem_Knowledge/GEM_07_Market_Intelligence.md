## WHAT CLIENTS ACTUALLY WANT (Hidden Needs)

### When the job says "generate images" → Hidden need: Brand consistency
They don't just want images. They want images that look like they belong to their brand. Mention style locking and multi-scene consistency.

### When the job says "high volume" → Hidden need: Reliability + system
They've been burned by freelancers who can't sustain output. Mention your automated pipeline and Airtable tracking.

### When the job says "no AI look" → Hidden need: Trust
They've tried AI before and been embarrassed by the result. Mention your 95% blind test rating and Photoshop finishing process.

### When the job says "consistent brand" → Hidden need: Process + documentation
They need a repeatable system, not a one-off output. Offer to build a brand DNA document as part of onboarding.

### When the job says "urgent" → Hidden need: Responsive communication
Speed matters, but so does communication. Mention your timezone overlap and 24-hour first-batch turnaround.

## RED FLAGS TO AVOID

| Red Flag | Why to Avoid |
|:---|:---|
| Rate below $10/hr for specialist work | Likely middleman HR cutting costs. Use counter-offer script. |
| "Test project" with no budget | Time-wasters. Always set minimum test rate or decline. |
| > 50 applicants + no payment verified | Very low win probability. Skip unless perfect Niche A fit. |
| Vague brief ("just make it look good") | Scope creep risk. Ask for detailed creative brief before starting. |
| "We've tried 5 other freelancers" | Client may be difficult. Ask why others didn't work out. |

---

## GREEN FLAGS (High Priority Jobs)

| Green Flag | Why It Matters |
|:---|:---|
| Payment verified + good client history | Lower risk of non-payment |
| Long-term / ongoing work implied | Higher lifetime value per connect spent |
| Specific niche mentioned (jewelry, skincare, food) | Better fit = better win rate |
| "We have a reference" or "we have assets" | Client is prepared = smoother project |
| Invite-only or direct message | 0 connects cost, higher win probability |
| Budget at or above $20/hr or $1,500+/month | Justifies senior-level positioning |
