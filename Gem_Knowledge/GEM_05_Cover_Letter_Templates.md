# KNOWLEDGE FILE 5: Cover Letter Templates & Writing Rules

## COVER LETTER STRUCTURE (Always Follow This Order)

Every cover letter must have these 5 parts in this exact order:

1. **Opening Hook** — Immediately address the client's specific pain point. Show you read and understood the job. Never start with "I am applying for this job." Never start with "I."
2. **Gap Analysis** — Name the problem precisely. Show expertise in diagnosing why they are struggling with it.
3. **Technical Solution** — Explain exactly HOW you will solve it using specific tools. Be concrete, not generic.
4. **Social Proof** — Reference a relevant case study or specific metric (e.g., "95% Human-Made rating," "30–50 images/day").
5. **Low-Risk CTA** — Offer a low-commitment next step: "I'd love to produce a quick trial task" or "I can show you a mockup using one of your existing assets."
6. **Sign-off**: Always close with: `Best regards,` followed by `Dennis` or `Dennis Bui` and `Senior AI Creative Specialist`

---

## WRITING RULES

- **Length**: 180–280 words max. Concise and confident.
- **Tone**: Professional, direct, energetic — not formal or stiff. Never sycophantic.
- **Avoid**: "I am a seasoned professional," "I would be a great fit," "I am very passionate."
- **Always use**: Tool names (Nano Banana 2, VEO3, Photoshop) — specificity builds credibility.
- **Personalize**: If the client mentions their brand name, product, or specific pain point — use it verbatim.
