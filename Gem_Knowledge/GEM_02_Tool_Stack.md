# KNOWLEDGE FILE 2: Full AI Tool Stack & Capabilities

## PRIMARY IMAGE GENERATION TOOLS

### Google Nano Banana 2 (NanoBanana 2 / Google Flow)
- **Level**: Expert
- **Use**: Primary image generation engine for all studio-grade product photography, ad creatives, and lifestyle staging.
- **Strengths**: 4K resolution output, photorealistic product rendering, consistent multi-image series, advanced style referencing.
- **How to reference in cover letters**: "Google Nano Banana 2" or "Google's advanced AI image generation engine."

### Google Omni
- **Level**: Expert
- **Use**: Multi-scene continuity — maintains product identity, lighting, and style across dozens of scene variations.
- **Strengths**: Themed scene generation, character consistency, multi-reference style locking.

### Google Flow (Tool Builder)
- **Level**: Advanced
- **Use**: Custom AI tool building for specialized pipelines (e.g., room design consistency, ControlNet-style viewpoint matching).

### Midjourney v6
- **Level**: Advanced
- **Use**: Photorealistic static Meta Ads, UGC aesthetics, styled fashion/lifestyle imagery.
- **Strengths**: The gold standard for precise styling, typography integration, and creative direction.

### Flux.1 (by Black Forest Labs)
- **Level**: Advanced
- **Use**: Prompt-adherence-critical tasks, text/logo on product, open-weight fine-tuning.
- **Strengths**: Best for legible text generation within images.

### ChatGPT Image Generation (GPT-4o)
- **Level**: Intermediate-Advanced
- **Use**: Rapid concept prototyping, reference image building, multi-modal research.
