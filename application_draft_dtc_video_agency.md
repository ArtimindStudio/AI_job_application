## Cover Letter

Hi,

I specialize in AI-native DTC eCommerce video production — exactly the kind of high-volume, high-quality output your agency needs across 10 brands.

**Why I can deliver 4 videos/day consistently:**

My workflow is built specifically for scale. For each video (3 hooks + 1 body), I follow this pipeline:

1. **Brief Intake & Strategy** — Using Antigravity, I map each brand's visual identity, tone, and winning ad angles once upfront. This eliminates guesswork on every subsequent video.

2. **Hook Generation (3 per video)** — Using Google VEO and KlingAI, I generate scroll-stopping 3-5 second hooks with different angles (pain point, curiosity, social proof) — each with product-accurate footage, dynamic camera movement, and punchy pacing.

3. **Body Production** — I combine AI-generated B-roll (product demos, lifestyle scenes, ingredient close-ups) with structured direct-response scripts using Runway for cinematic motion and transitions.

4. **Assembly & Polish** — Final editing in Premiere Pro/After Effects for pacing, text overlays, sound design, and platform-specific formatting.

**What makes this sustainable at volume:**
- I maintain a visual asset library per brand, so recurring production gets faster over time
- My AI tools handle the heavy lifting on footage generation — I focus on strategy and finishing
- I understand DTC performance creative: hook rates, thumb-stop, benefit stacking, CTA urgency

I'm available to start ASAP and can handle 40 hours/week. Happy to do a paid test day (4 videos) so you can evaluate output quality and turnaround speed firsthand.

Portfolio samples attached.

Best,
Dennis
